### Exercise 13. Fibonacci matrix

Given

$$
F=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
1 & 0 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
F^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F^3=
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 2 \\
2 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
F^4=
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 3 \\
3 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F^5=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 5 \\
5 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

For a vector $(a,b)^T$,

$$
F
\begin{pmatrix}
a \\
b \\
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
a+b \\
a \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

This transformation generates the Fibonacci recurrence.
