### Exercise 11. Find all matrices that commute with a given matrix

Let

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 \\
0 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Then

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
a+c & b+d \\
c & d \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
BA=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & a+b \\
c & c+d \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Setting $AB=BA$ gives $c=0$ and $d=a$. Hence

$$
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
0 & a \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are arbitrary scalars.
