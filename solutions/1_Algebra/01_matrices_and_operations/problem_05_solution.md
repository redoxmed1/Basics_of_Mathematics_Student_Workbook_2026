### Exercise 5. Matrix-vector multiplication and linear combinations

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\
1 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
Ax=
\begin{pmatrix}
2(4)+(-1)(2) \\
1(4)+3(2) \\
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Equivalently, $Ax=4a_1+2a_2$, where $a_1$ and $a_2$ are the columns of $A$.
