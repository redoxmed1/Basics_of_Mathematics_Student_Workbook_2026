### Exercise 4. Matrix multiplication is not commutative

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 0 \\
3 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 2 \\
3 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
BA=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 \\
3 & 7 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Since $AB\ne BA$, matrix multiplication is not commutative in general.
