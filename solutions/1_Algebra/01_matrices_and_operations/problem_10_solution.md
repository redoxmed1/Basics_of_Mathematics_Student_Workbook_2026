### Exercise 10. Product by columns

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
-1 & 0 \\
3 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Let the columns of $B$ be $b_1$ and $b_2$. Then

$$
Ab_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Ab_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Therefore,

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 & 2 \\
2 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
