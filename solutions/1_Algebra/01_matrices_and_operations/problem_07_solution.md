### Exercise 7. Powers of a matrix

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The identity and zero matrix properties give $AI=IA=A$ and $A+0=A$.

$$
A^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 4 \\
0 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A^3=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 12 \\
0 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The pattern is

$$
A^n=
\begin{pmatrix}
2^n & n2^{n-1} \\
0 & 2^n \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
