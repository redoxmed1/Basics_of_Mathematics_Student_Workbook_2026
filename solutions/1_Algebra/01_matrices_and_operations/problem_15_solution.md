### Exercise 15. Associativity of matrix multiplication

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\quad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
2 & 1 \\
-1 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\quad
C=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & -1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

First,

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 2 \\
1 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Then,

$$
(AB)C=
\begin{pmatrix}
10 & 3 \\
2 & -3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Next,

$$
BC=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
4 & 1 \\
-2 & -4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Therefore,

$$
A(BC)=
\begin{pmatrix}
10 & 3 \\
2 & -3 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Since $(AB)C=A(BC)$, the products verify associativity for these matrices.
