### Exercise 14. Rotation matrices

The rotation matrix is

$$
R(\alpha)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\alpha & -\sin\alpha \\
\sin\alpha & \cos\alpha \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Multiplying $R(\alpha)$ and $R(\beta)$, and using the angle-addition identities, gives

$$
R(\alpha)R(\beta)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\alpha+\beta) & -\sin(\alpha+\beta) \\
\sin(\alpha+\beta) & \cos(\alpha+\beta) \\
\end{pmatrix}
=R(\alpha+\beta)
$$

Therefore, composing two rotations adds their angles.
