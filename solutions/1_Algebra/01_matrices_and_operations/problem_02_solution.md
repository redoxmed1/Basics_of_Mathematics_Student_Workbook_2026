### Exercise 2. Matrix addition and scalar multiplication

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
-1 & 3 \\
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & -2 \\
0 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 0 \\
-1 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & 4 \\
-1 & -2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
3A-2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-5 & 10 \\
-3 & -1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
